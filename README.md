# Crisis Preparedness — Panama

A living, source-grounded archive on crisis and disaster preparedness in Panama: what can happen, what has already happened, how to prepare, how to respond, and who to contact.

Built for people living in Panama (and anyone interested), with a focus on real, local, current information — real prices, real agencies, real history — rather than generic advice. Every factual claim is cited to a source, and contested or unverified figures are stated openly rather than smoothed over.

## What's inside

| Folder | Topic |
|---|---|
| [`events/`](events/) | What has happened and what could happen — the 2026 earthquake, Cobre Panamá protests, COVID-19, Canal drought, cyberattacks, future risk scenarios, a deep dive on cyber/AI risk to the money system, and how the two world wars affected Panama and Latin America |
| [`hazards/`](hazards/) | Eight hazard profiles — earthquake, flood, drought, wildfire, storm, epidemic, cyber, civil unrest |
| [`preparedness/`](preparedness/) | Plans, kits, checklists, and supplies with real local prices — water, food, first aid, shelter, jungle survival, go-bag, home, budgets, and surviving weeks without internet |
| [`human-behavior/`](human-behavior/) | How people actually behave in disasters, starvation, and war — the psychology plus true survivor stories |

## How it's organized

- Every topic lives in its own subfolder.
- Every subfolder has a `README.md` that acts as a **routing map** — it lists the files inside and what each holds, so you (or an AI assistant) can get an overview without reading everything.
- Read a subfolder's README first, then open only the specific file(s) you need.
- Files are plain Markdown. Names, dates, and figures are written exactly as sourced; unverifiable prices are marked rather than guessed.

## Roadmap

Planned additions: `response/` (what to do during and after an event), `authorities/` (agencies, alert systems, emergency contacts), `legal/` (emergency law and rights), `resources/` (external sources).

## Status

Started October 2026 and actively maintained. Most files are complete with sources; a few areas (older historical events) still need expansion and fact-checking.
