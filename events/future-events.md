# Future Events / Risk Scenarios (Panama)

Possible events that could happen in Panama, and how to think about each.
Ordered by priority, with likelihood, affected areas, and preparedness implications.

---

## 1. Global or large-scale cyberattack

- **Hazard type:** cyber — ransomware, critical-infrastructure compromise, coordinated denial-of-service, data breach
- **Likelihood:** high and rising. Precedent already exists in Panama: the MEF breach claimed by INC Ransom (Sep 2025), a MINSA platform hack (Sep 2025), and 31.5m attacks against the Electoral Tribunal on a single election day (2024).
- **Affected areas:** government services, the Ministry of Economy & Finance (which handles Canal revenues), health systems, utilities, banks, and elections — nationwide, not geographic.
- **Early warning / lead time:** none — attacks are sudden; only monitoring and readiness help.
- **Primary risks:** suspension of payments/benefits and government services, theft of citizen and fiscal data, loss of public trust, prolonged outages, and knock-on effects if Canal-related financial systems are hit.
- **Preparedness implications:** offline backups of personal documents and contacts; a cash reserve (a banking/payment outage can last days); know official (non-internet) channels for alerts; basic digital hygiene (updates, MFA, no reused passwords); verify any payment instruction by an independent channel (the deepfake-fraud lesson); assume a big attack is a "when, not if".
- **Deep dive** (what's documented, what regulators warn, what tech leaders say, realistic "AI gone rogue on the money system" scenarios) → `cyber-ai-finance.md`
- **Sources:** BleepingComputer (MEF), MINSA statement, La Prensa (Electoral Tribunal) — see `past-events.md` → "2025 — Cyber incidents".

## 2. Another major earthquake

- **Hazard type:** earthquake (shallow strike-slip on the Azuero-Soná fault system, or the frequent Chiriquí border seismicity)
- **Likelihood:** high. Panama sits on the Pacific "Ring of Fire"; the Azuero-Soná fault system is active, and the 9 Oct 2026 M7.7 event is a reminder that large shallow inland quakes do occur. Aftershocks of the 2026 sequence are still ongoing.
- **Affected areas:** central provinces (Azuero, Herrera, Veraguas, Coclé, Los Santos) for the Azuero-Soná fault; western Chiriquí for border events; felt nationwide.
- **Early warning / lead time:** seconds to none for earthquakes; minutes for a tsunami warning after an offshore/coastal event.
- **Primary risks:** building collapse, road/landslide isolation of communities, water/power outages (as seen in Tonosí, 2026), and tsunami risk on the Pacific coast.
- **Preparedness implications:** earthquake kit (water, food, first aid, flashlight, radio, documents); a family evacuation and meeting plan; know the tsunami-evacuation route if you are coastal; secure tall furniture; post-2026 building-safety assessments should be acted on, not deferred.
- **Sources:** USGS 2026 event page, Wikipedia 2026 earthquake, ReliefWeb — see `past-events.md` → "2026-10-09".

## 3. New pandemic

- **Hazard type:** epidemic / public-health emergency
- **Likelihood:** medium-to-high long-term (zoonotic spillover and climate-driven disease spread are rising globally); COVID-19 showed Panama's exposure.
- **Affected areas:** nationwide; concentrated in dense Panama City/Colón, with the interior vulnerable to supply-chain and healthcare gaps.
- **Early warning / lead time:** weeks to months of international warning before local spread — Panama's 2020 response began quickly after the first confirmed case.
- **Primary risks:** health-system overload, supply disruption, deep economic contraction (Panama's 2020–21 lockdown was among the region's strictest and most costly).
- **Preparedness implications:** keep a basic stockpile of food, medicine, and hygiene items; maintain remote-work/school capability; know your local health-authority (MINSA) channels; a non-discriminatory, clearly-communicated rule set matters (the 2020 gender-day quarantine drew criticism).
- **Sources:** Wikipedia COVID-19 in Panama, PMC peer-reviewed data — see `past-events.md` → "2020–2023".

## 4. Renewed drought / Canal disruption (already underway)

- **Hazard type:** drought / water crisis (economic + water security)
- **Likelihood:** high — already happening: the Canal is capping daily transits (34 from 4 Sep 2026, 32 from 15 Sep 2026) as an El Niño event strengthens; May–Aug 2026 rainfall was 34% below average and inflows 44% below normal.
- **Affected areas:** Canal watershed and Gatún Lake (also a drinking-water source for Panama City and Colón), plus the global shipping and local economy.
- **Early warning / lead time:** months — rainfall and El Niño forecasts give advance notice.
- **Primary risks:** lower Canal revenue, shipping delays, cargo/price effects, and reduced drinking-water reserves.
- **Preparedness implications:** this is a recurring, predictable stress, not a surprise — household water conservation, awareness of possible water-rationing measures, and business continuity for anyone dependent on Canal logistics.
- **Sources:** Reuters (Aug 2026 transit caps), gCaptain (El Niño watch), Woodwell (2023–24 drought) — see `past-events.md` → "2023–2024".

## 5. Renewed civil unrest / political crisis

- **Hazard type:** civil unrest / economic crisis
- **Likelihood:** medium — the Cobre Panamá mine's future is undecided (government decision expected late 2026); cost-of-living pressures have triggered blockades before (mine protests 2023, fuel-price protests 2022).
- **Affected areas:** nationwide, concentrated along the Pan-American Highway and other chokepoints; Panama City and Colón most affected economically.
- **Early warning / lead time:** days to weeks — disputes are usually announced in advance and build over time.
- **Primary risks:** road blockades cutting supply chains and travel, business closures, and escalation to confrontation.
- **Preparedness implications:** monitor news around the mine decision and any subsidy/fuel price moves; keep food, fuel and cash buffers if you depend on road transport; avoid protest areas.
- **Sources:** mining.com / Yahoo Finance / Newsroom Panama (mine dispute status) — see `past-events.md` → "2023".
