# Civil unrest & blockades (Panama)

Cost-of-living and policy grievances can escalate into nationwide protests and road blockades that cut supply chains.

## Where & when

- Nationwide, concentrated on the Pan-American Highway and other chokepoints; Panama City and Colón for economic impact.
- Builds over days/weeks after a triggering policy decision.

## Key events

- 2023 Cobre Panamá protests — the largest in recent history (Law 406 → unconstitutional → mine closed). See `../events/past-events.md`.

## Risk outlook

Medium — the mine's future decision (expected late 2026) is a live flashpoint. See `../events/future-events.md` (scenario #5).

## Preparedness essentials

- Monitor news around the mine decision and any fuel/subsidy price moves.
- Keep food, fuel and cash buffers if you depend on road transport; avoid protest areas.

## Authorities & alerts

- No single authority; follow news and SINAPROC advisories.
