# Cyberattacks (Panama)

Rising threat to government, finance and elections. The Ministry of Economy & Finance handles Canal revenues, making it a high-value target.

## Where & when

- No seasonality; ransomware and state-linked groups active year-round.

## Key events

- 2025-09: MEF breach claimed by INC Ransom (1.5 TB allegedly stolen).
- 2025-09: MINSA platform unauthorised access (no patient data).
- 2024-05 election day: 31.5m attacks against the Electoral Tribunal (all blocked).

## Risk outlook

High — the top-priority future scenario. See `../events/future-events.md` (scenario #1) and the deep dive `../events/cyber-ai-finance.md` (AI + money system, tech-leader statements, realistic rogue-AI scenarios). For preparing for weeks without internet, see `../preparedness/no-internet.md`.

## Preparedness essentials

- Offline backups of documents and contacts; a cash reserve for payment outages.
- Digital hygiene: updates, MFA, unique passwords.
- Know official (non-internet) alert channels.

## Authorities & alerts

- AIG (Autoridad Nacional para la Innovación Gubernamental); Procuraduría General (cybercrime)
