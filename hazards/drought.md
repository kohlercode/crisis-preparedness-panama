# Drought / water crisis (Panama)

El Niño drives drought that hits both the Panama Canal (shipping and revenue) and drinking-water supply — Gatún Lake feeds Panama City and Colón.

## Where & when

- El Niño years; driest months January–April.
- The Canal watershed (Gatún Lake) is the critical node.

## Key events

- 2023–24: transits cut from ~38 to 24/day; driest October since 1950; record-low Gatún Lake on 1 Jan 2024.
- 2026: a new El Niño — Canal capping transits (34 from 4 Sep, 32 from 15 Sep).

## Risk outlook

High and recurring, predictable months ahead. See `../events/future-events.md` (scenario #4).

## Preparedness essentials

- Household water conservation; expect possible rationing during El Niño.
- Business continuity planning if you depend on Canal logistics.

## Authorities & alerts

- Panama Canal Authority (ACP); ETESA; IDAAN (water utility)
