# Earthquake (Panama)

Panama sits on the Pacific "Ring of Fire". Two seismic settings dominate: frequent moderate quakes in western Chiriquí (near the Costa Rica border), and larger shallow inland quakes on the Azuero-Soná fault system crossing the Azuero Peninsula.

## Where & when

- No seasonality — earthquakes can occur at any time.
- Chiriquí / Panama–Costa Rica border: frequent moderate events (M5–6).
- Azuero-Soná fault (Herrera, Los Santos, Veraguas): rarer but larger shallow events; the 2026 M7.7 struck here.
- Shaking is felt nationwide for larger events; tsunami risk is on the Pacific coast.

## Key events

- 2026-10-09 M7.7 — state of emergency; 2 dead, 27 injured → see `../events/past-events.md`
- 2025-07-14 M6.2 and 2025-03-21 M6.2 — no damage reported
- 2024-07-22 M5.7 (Puerto Armuelles) and 2024-07-08 M5.3 (Chiriquí, 24 homes)
- Historical: 1882 San Blas M8.3 (250 dead), 1991 Limón M7.7 (75–87 dead), 2003 Puerto Armuelles M6.5 (2 dead) — see the table in `../events/past-events.md`

## Risk outlook

High — another major quake is a question of when, not if. See `../events/future-events.md` (scenario #2).

## Preparedness essentials

- Earthquake kit: water, food, first aid, flashlight, radio, copies of documents, cash.
- Practise drop/cover/hold; secure tall furniture and water heaters.
- Know your evacuation route and a family meeting point; if coastal, know the tsunami-evacuation route.
- Expect aftershocks for days to weeks and plan to sleep away from damaged structures if necessary.
- Act on post-earthquake building-safety assessments rather than deferring repairs.

## Authorities & alerts

- SINAPROC (Sistema Nacional de Protección Civil) — civil protection and alerts
- Instituto de Geociencias, Universidad de Panamá (IGC-UP) — national seismology
- USGS — event details; tsunami.gov — Pacific tsunami warnings
