# Epidemics & pandemics (Panama)

COVID-19 was the defining recent public-health emergency; dengue, Zika and other vector-borne diseases are endemic threats.

## Where & when

- Pandemics: global, reaching Panama quickly via Tocumen and other hubs (first COVID case 9 Mar 2020).
- Dengue: endemic, peaking in the rainy season.

## Key events

- 2020–23 COVID-19: 1,032,316 cases and 8,621 deaths; one of the region's strictest lockdowns (gender-based quarantine). See `../events/past-events.md`.

## Risk outlook

New pandemic: medium–high long-term. See `../events/future-events.md` (scenario #3).

## Preparedness essentials

- Basic stockpile (food, medicine, hygiene); remote-work/school capability.
- Follow MINSA/PAHO channels.

## Authorities & alerts

- MINSA (Ministerio de Salud); PAHO/WHO
