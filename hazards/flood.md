# Floods & landslides (Panama)

Heavy wet-season rains (May–December) trigger flash floods and landslides, worst in the western and Caribbean provinces (Chiriquí, Bocas del Toro, Veraguas, Colón, Panama Oeste).

## Where & when

- Rainy season May–December; peak October–November.
- Chiriquí and Bocas del Toro are the most flood-prone provinces.
- Landslides follow heavy rain, especially on steep or deforested slopes.

## Key events

- 2024-11: severe floods/landslides in Chiriquí and Bocas del Toro — at least 4 dead, 200+ families affected.
- 2021-11-21: flash floods — at least 2 dead, 1 missing.
- 2020-11: heavy rain and landslides in Chiriquí.
- Post-earthquake rain in Oct 2026 triggered landslides and yellow alerts in Veraguas/Los Santos (see `../events/past-events.md`).

## Risk outlook

Recurring every wet season; rain now overlaps with earthquake displacement. See `../events/future-events.md`.

## Preparedness essentials

- Know whether your area floods; never cross flooded roads or rivers.
- Keep an emergency kit; monitor weather alerts during Oct–Nov.
- On slopes, watch for early landslide signs (cracks, tilting trees).

## Authorities & alerts

- SINAPROC; ETESA (hydrometeorology forecasts/warnings); Red Cross / IFRC
