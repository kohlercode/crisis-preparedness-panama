# Tropical cyclones (Panama)

Panama lies south of the main hurricane belt, so direct hits are rare, but the outer bands of Caribbean hurricanes and Pacific storms bring heavy rain, wind and landslides.

## Where & when

- Atlantic hurricane season June–November.
- Caribbean coast (Bocas del Toro, Colón) and Panama City most exposed to outer-band effects.

## Key events

- 2016-11 — Hurricane Otto (Cat 3): at least 9 dead in Panama; high winds downed trees (a falling tree killed a child in Panama City); landslides killed three. First Atlantic→Pacific crossover since 1996.

## Risk outlook

Low–moderate for a direct hit; indirect rain/flood/wind impacts are the real risk. See `../events/future-events.md`.

## Preparedness essentials

- Same as floods, plus secure loose outdoor objects during high-wind warnings.

## Authorities & alerts

- SINAPROC; ETESA; NOAA/NHC (storm tracking)
