# Wildfires (Panama)

Dry-season vegetation fires (locally "incendios de masa vegetal" / IMAVES) burn large areas annually; ~90% are human-caused (agricultural burning, negligence).

## Where & when

- Dry season January–April.
- Nationwide, concentrated in rural and agricultural areas.

## Key events

- 2012–2022: 16,099 fires affecting 229,528.9 hectares (MiAmbiente).

## Risk outlook

Seasonal and chronic; worsened by drought and heat. See `../events/future-events.md`.

## Preparedness essentials

- Avoid open burning in the dry season; clear vegetation near structures.
- Report fires early to the fire brigade (Cuerpo de Bomberos).

## Authorities & alerts

- MiAmbiente (Ministerio de Ambiente); Cuerpo de Bomberos; SINAPROC
