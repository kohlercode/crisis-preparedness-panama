# First aid & medications (Panama)

## The kit (SINAPROC "botiquín familiar")

Official SINAPROC first-aid kit contents: **gasa, vendas, alcohol o agua oxigenada, paletas de madera (splints), curitas, algodón, guantes de látex, mascarillas desechables**, plus a first-aid manual and **each family member's specific medications + prescriptions**.

## Cheaper generic option

MINSA runs a "medicamentos más baratos" list: **paracetamol ~$0.07/tablet** and **ibuprofen ~$0.23/tablet** at participating pharmacies — ask the pharmacist for the cheapest generic, not the brand.

## Notes

- **Rehydration salts (suero oral) are the highest-value item** in a tropical emergency — dehydration from heat or diarrhoea kills faster than anything else on this list. Buy several sachets.
- Insect repellent + sunscreen matter year-round in Panama (dengue is endemic).
- Keep **prescriptions + a written list of each member's medications** in the kit (SINAPROC).
- If anyone takes daily medication, keep a 1–2 week extra supply — a road closure or cyber outage can interrupt pharmacies.
