# The evacuation bag — "mochila de 72 horas" (Panama)

SINAPROC's official 72-hour kit, plus the practical version.

## Official contents (SINAPROC mochila de 72 horas)

- **Water** — a 3-day supply (amount depends on family: children, elderly, special needs).
- **Food** — canned, sometimes dehydrated, easy to open and prepare.
- **Clothes & hygiene** — three changes (sweater, shoes, pants, hat, blankets; baby and elderly clothing) + hygiene items.
- **First-aid kit** — gasa, vendas, alcohol/agua oxigenada, splints, curitas, algodón, guantes de látex, mascarillas, a manual, and each member's medications + prescriptions.
- **Tools** — radio with batteries, flashlights with batteries, strong rope, protected matches, whistle.
- **Phone directory** — notebook with emergency numbers, doctor, family, the children's school.
- **Important documents** — marriage certificate, **cédulas** (ID), children's juvenile ID, prescriptions, ID tags for each member — in a plastic bag.
- **Keys** — spare keys near the door + a copy in the kit.
- **Tool box** — hammer, pliers, nails, trowel, screws, wall plugs.

## Practical additions

- **Cash in small bills (USD)** — ATMs/cards fail in a cyber or power outage.
- **Power bank** + charging cable.
- **Copies of documents** (also store digital copies in the cloud or with a friend abroad).
- A small supply of any daily medication (1–2 weeks).

## Assemble it cheaply

Use a sturdy backpack you already own. The cheapest complete version (see `budgets.md` "Básico" tier) is roughly **$50–55** for a family. Keep it near the door; update the food/water/meds every few months.
