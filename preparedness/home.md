# Home hardening (Panama)

What to do in advance, by hazard. All from SINAPROC guidance.

## Earthquake ("sismos")

- Know your building: **adobe/self-built → evacuate immediately** during shaking; engineered structures → shelter in place (drop/cover/hold). Strict building codes spared the high-rises in the 2026 M7.7 quake.
- Identify and **secure tall furniture, water heaters, and objects that can fall**.
- Know where the **gas, water, electricity cut-offs** are and how to shut them.
- Anchor the gas cylinder (a common danger in Panamanian homes).

## Flood ("inundación")

- Identify safe (high) and risky areas; know your route to higher ground.
- Store documents in plastic bags (part of the kit).
- During: cut the electrical panel and disconnect the gas tank; go to high ground; **never cross a swollen river or walk its banks**; stay away from downed power lines.

## Hurricane / storm

- Secure roofs, doors, windows, and loose objects; clear gutters/drains.
- During lightning: stay away from trees, metal structures, and billboards; seek safe shelter.
- Follow SINAPROC "avisos de prevención" before storms.

## Fire

- Keep a fire extinguisher accessible and know how to use it; don't overload outlets (a listed SINAPROC home risk).

## After any event

- Check for gas leaks before using lighters/matches; use flashlights.
- Don't re-enter buildings until checked; drink only bottled or boiled water; await official guidance.
