# Household emergency plan (Panama)

The foundation. Do this first — the kits only help if everyone knows what to do.

## 1. The plan (SINAPROC "Plan de Emergencia Familiar")

SINAPROC defines this as a drawn map, done in four steps:

1. **Map the home.** Draw your house from above; mark the dangers (steep stairs, gas cylinder, electrical points) and the evacuation route to the nearest low-risk point. Identify an external **meeting point** (punto de encuentro) and a **safe zone** (zona de seguridad). List your local threats: sismos, incendios, erupciones volcánicas, inundaciones, deslizamientos, otros.
2. **Review** it with the family; update when the home or household changes, or yearly.
3. **Post** it where everyone (including children) can see it.
4. **Drill** — run a quick evacuation and time it, then fix what failed.

## 2. Assign roles

Who grabs the go-bag, who grabs the documents, who helps children/elderly/disabled, who shuts off gas/electricity/water. Everyone should know where the shut-off valves and breakers are.

## 3. Emergency contacts directory

Keep a paper notebook (SINAPROC recommends it) — phones and power may fail:

| Service | Number |
|---|---|
| Emergencias / ambulancia (SUME) | **911** |
| Policía Nacional | **104** |
| Cuerpo de Bomberos | **103** |
| SINAPROC (Protección Civil) | **520-4426** (también *135) |
| Cruz Roja Panameña | ***455** |
| SENAFRONT (fronteras) | **109** |

Also write: family members' numbers, the children's school, your doctor, and one out-of-town contact (in a disaster it's often easier to reach someone outside the affected area).

## 4. The alert system (colors)

SINAPROC alert levels:

- **Verde** — permanent monitoring of risk areas (vigilance/prevention).
- **Alerta Temprana Preventiva** — a reinforced green state; tighter monitoring of a detected risk.
- **Amarilla** — a threat is growing and may exceed local resources; resources are readied.
- **Roja** — a threat has grown; mobilise all available resources.

For you: **amarilla = act now** (top up water, fuel, food, charge devices); **roja = execute the plan** (evacuate if ordered).

## 5. Where to get official information

- SINAPROC (sinaproc.gob.pa) and its official social channels — follow official accounts only; don't spread unverified chains.
- Weather advisories: ETESA / IMHPA.
