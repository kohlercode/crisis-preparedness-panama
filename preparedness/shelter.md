The equipment companion to `supplies.md` (which covers light/power/comms). This file covers what you need to **build, repair, and sleep in shelter** and to work in the field — with real Panamanian retail prices (USD, Oct 2026). The single most useful item here is the **tarp (lona)**; the single most Panamanian item is the **machete**.

## Building a basic tarp shelter

1. **Ridgeline:** tie rope between two trees at ~waist-to-shoulder height, tight.
2. **A-frame:** drape the tarp over the ridgeline, peg the four corners with stakes (or heavy rocks/logs in rocky soil).
3. **Tropics priorities, in order:** (1) **rain** — slope the tarp so water runs off, don't let it sag into pools; (2) **insects** — a net is separate and essential (see `jungle.md`); (3) **off the ground** — flood and snakes.
4. Lash poles/bamboo with zip ties or wire where trees are absent.
