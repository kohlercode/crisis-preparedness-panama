## What to get first (in order)

1. **Flashlight + batteries** — a real flashlight beats candles. SINAPROC: after an earthquake, **use flashlights, not candles/matches/lighters** (gas-leak fire risk).
2. **Candles + matches** — cheap backup for long outages.
3. **Power bank** — phones are how you get alerts and reach family; a charged 10,000 mAh bank recharges a phone several times.
4. **Radio** — official information when internet/cell towers are down (the $29 solar+crank radio is the single best comms prep).
5. **Whistle + duct tape + tarp + rope** — signalling and basic shelter/repair.

## Low-cost note

The $0.72 Hopsa flashlight, a box of candles ($1.35–2.79), matches ($0.49) and a whistle ($2.39) give you light and signalling for **under $5**. Add the $17.95 power bank when you can.
