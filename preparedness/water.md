# Water (Panama)

Water is the single most important prep — and the cheapest to get right in Panama because of the garrafón (5-gallon jug) culture.

## How much

- Minimum: **1 gallon (3.79 L) per person per day** for drinking + basic hygiene.
- SINAPROC recommends a 3-day supply in the 72-hour kit; build toward 7–14 days.

## Tap-water reality

- Panama City tap water is generally **safe to drink** (heavily chlorinated); IDAAN treats ~70% of the country. Exceptions: **Bocas del Toro** and **Guna Yala** — don't drink the tap there.
- **Reliability is the problem, not quality:** 20+ communities in Panama City/San Miguelito get water only 4–6 hours/day (low pressure, early morning). IDAAN rations in Volcán (Chiriquí) during El Niño; some plants run at reduced capacity.
- Most homes have a **tanque de reserva** (reserve tank). Keep it clean and topped up — you fill it during short supply windows and live off it during longer cuts.

## Storage (cheap → better)

- **Free/cheapest:** reuse clean food-grade bottles (2–3 L soda bottles) filled from the tap.
- **Best value:** garrafón 5-gal refill — **$4.99** (Purissima, Novey) ≈ **$0.26/L**. Reuse the jug. This is the cheapest drinking water in Panama.
- Buy sealed garrafones/jugs rather than small bottles; store them cool and dark.

## Purification (if stored water runs out)

- **Unscented bleach (cloro):** Clorox Original 1 galón — **$3.66** (label: "apto para potabilizar agua"). Dose (CDC): 2 drops per litre of clear water, wait 30 min. **Must be plain/unscented** — not the "pureza cítrica" kind.
- **Purification tablets (Aquatabs 67 mg):** sold by Ecovital (Vía España, Panama City); one tablet treats ~10 L. **Price not published (phone/WhatsApp order) — not found, ask the distributor.**
- **Boiling:** 1 minute rolling boil (3 min at high altitude) — free, always works.
- **Chlor-Floc kit** (5 sachets + 1 L container): $6.99 (Camping Store Panama).

## Low-cost rule of thumb

Three clean 2 L bottles per person = ~6 L/person of storage for nearly free. Add one garrafón ($4.99) for drinking water, and keep a small bottle of unscented bleach ($3.66/gal) as your purification backup. A family's basic water security for **under $10**.
